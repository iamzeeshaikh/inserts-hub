# Migration conflicts and judgement calls

Conflicts between legitimate sources were resolved in this order:

1. Current legitimate, uncontaminated live page
2. WordPress XML export
3. WooCommerce CSV export
4. Elementor JSON
5. Screenshot / PDF

Malware-contaminated live content never overrode a clean export.

---

## 1. Which homepage template is real

**Conflict.** The export contains six homepage-ish Elementor templates: #36
`homepage`, #494 `Homepage`, #7057 `Homepage`, #7060 `latest homepage`, #7063
`other`, plus slides #2722/#2730/#2736.

Template #36 is a complete **The Candle Packaging** homepage, not an Inserts Hub
one.

**Resolution.** The supplied full-page PDF capture (3 August 2026) and the live
homepage HTML are the authority for what actually renders. Both show: a hero
slider, four feature cards, a 31-product grid with a sidebar enquiry form, and a
red quote CTA. Template #36 renders nowhere and was excluded.

**Evidence.** Slides #2722/#2730/#2736 contain exactly the three headings the
rebuilt slider uses, and #2722 carries the `<h1>`.

---

## 2. Homepage product grid: 18 products in the XML vs 31 live

**Conflict.** The rendered homepage snapshot inside the WordPress XML lists 18
products. The PDF capture and the live page show 31.

**Resolution.** The XML snapshot is stale — it predates the five `Cardboard
Inserts` products added in July 2026. The PDF's 31-product order was used, which
matches the 31 published products in the WooCommerce CSV and the 31 product URLs
in the live `product-sitemap.xml` exactly.

---

## 3. Fabricated Product review data

**Conflict.** Every live product page emits:

```json
"aggregateRating": { "ratingValue": "5", "reviewCount": 1 },
"review": [{ "author": { "name": "shanimazhar82@gmail.com" },
             "description": "<the page's meta description>" }]
```

The "reviewer" is the site administrator's own login email, and the review body
is a copy of the meta description. Customer reviews are also disabled on every
product (`Allow customer reviews? = 0` in the CSV), so no real review exists.

**Resolution.** **Not carried over.** This is invented structured data, it is
self-serving review markup that Google's guidelines disallow, and the brief
explicitly forbids inventing aggregate ratings or reviews. Everything else in the
Offer — price, currency, availability, SKU, MPN, brand, images — is preserved.

**Impact.** Star ratings will disappear from any rich result that still shows
them. This is a deliberate correction, not a regression.

## 4. `priceValidUntil`

**Conflict.** Live Offer schema carries `priceValidUntil: 2027-07-23`, a rolling
date generated by the plugin with no basis in the product data.

**Resolution.** Omitted. Generating a validity date is inventing a fact. Price
(`0.3`), currency (`USD`) and availability (`InStock`) are preserved verbatim.

## 5. WooCommerce cart, checkout and My Account

**Conflict.** `/cart/`, `/checkout/` and `/my-account/` are in the live sitemap,
but they are WooCommerce runtime pages with no static equivalent — and all three
were carrying injected gambling spam.

**Resolution.** 301 to `/products/`, `/products/` and `/contact-us/`
respectively. Inserts Hub converts through the quote form, not a checkout: the
homepage CTA is "GET A CUSTOM QUOTE FOR INSERTS", every product page carries a
quote form, and the $0.30 unit price is indicative rather than transactional.

**⚠️ This is the one decision that changes site functionality.** If you do intend
to take card payments, the cart flow needs to be rebuilt on a payment provider —
say so and it can be added. See "Remaining manual decisions" in
`MIGRATION_REPORT.md`.

## 6. "Add To Cart" button on product pages

**Conflict.** Live product pages show an **Add To Cart** button linking to
`?add-to-cart=<id>`. With no cart, that link cannot survive.

**Resolution.** The button's slot is now occupied by the product enquiry form
that already sat directly beneath it on the live page, with the correct product
preselected. Commercial intent and the primary conversion path are preserved; a
button that would 404 was not.

---

## 7. Two About pages

**Conflict.** `/about-us/` (post #429) and `/about-us-2/` (post #3130) share the
same title, the same H1 and near-identical body copy. Both self-canonicalise and
both are in the live sitemap.

**Resolution.** `/about-us/` is the real page — it is the one wired into the main
menu and the footer menu. `/about-us-2/` is an orphan duplicate that also carried
injected casino spam. 301 to `/about-us/`.

## 8. `/product/White-cardboard-inserts/` served at HTTP 200

**Conflict.** `/product/kraft-cardboard-inserts/` links to a capitalised variant
of the White Cardboard Inserts URL. WordPress serves it at 200, creating
duplicate content.

**Resolution.** The contextual link was corrected to the canonical lowercase URL,
and the capitalised path 301s to it.

## 9. Links to two draft products

**Conflict.** Live copy links to `/product/printed-cardboard-inserts/` and
`/product/recycled-cardboard-inserts/`. Both are drafts (`Published = -1`) and
both **404 on the live site today** — broken links shipped to users and crawlers.

**Resolution.** Not created. Inbound links repointed to the closest live product
(Custom Cardboard Inserts and Custom Kraft Paper Inserts), and 301s configured
for the URLs themselves.

## 10. Pages with no `<h1>`

**Conflict.** The live homepage, contact page, refund page and thank-you page
render no `<h1>` at all.

**Resolution.** Each now has exactly one, using wording already on the page:

| Page | H1 | Source of the wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Custom Inserts for Every Need | Elementor slide #2722, already marked `<h1>` |
| `/contact-us/` | We're Here to Help! | The page's existing lead line, promoted from a styled paragraph |
| `/refund_returns/` | Refund and Returns Policy | The page title |
| `/thank-you/` | Thank you | The existing on-page heading (was an `<h2>`) |

No new copy was written.

## 11. Missing hero slide images

**Conflict.** Elementor slides reference `Corrugated-Cardboard-Inserts-Packaging-1.jpg`,
`Mini-Cupcake-Inserts-1.jpg` and `Cardboard-Divider-Inserts-Packaging-1.jpg`.
All three **404** — they were replaced by `.webp` versions during a media cleanup
and the slide references were never updated.

**Resolution.** Used the current `.webp` files of the same subjects from the
site's own media library (`Corrugated-Cardboard-Inserts-Packaging.webp`,
`Mini-Cupcake-Inserts.webp`, `Cardboard-Divider-Inserts-Packaging.webp`). No
photography was invented and no third-party image was introduced.

---

## 12. WooCommerce sorting dropdown

**Conflict.** The live archive has a "Default sorting / by popularity / by
latest / by price" dropdown producing `?orderby=` URLs.

**Resolution.** Not reproduced. It requires server-side state, adds no organic
value, and the brief requires query-string URLs to stay out of the sitemap.
Alphabetical order (the live default) and crawlable `/page/N/` pagination are
preserved exactly.

---

## 13. Meta descriptions that did not exist

**Conflict.** Seven pages had **no** meta description on the old site:
`/products/`, `/about-us/`, `/privacy-policy/`, `/refund_returns/`,
`/terms-conditions/`, `/thank-you/` and `/product-category/product/`.

**Resolution.** Unique, page-specific descriptions were written for each. A
missing description is a defect, not a ranking asset — and duplicate-description
checks require every page to have one. All 31 product descriptions, plus the
homepage, contact page and Cardboard Inserts category, are preserved verbatim
from Yoast.
